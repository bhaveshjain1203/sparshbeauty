Why Choose Sparsh Beauty Academy

Real Brides, Real Skin Tones — Not Just Textbook Theory

The first question before joining any makeup academy is simple: Who is teaching you, and how do their looks perform on real brides?

Many courses focus only on studio ring-light photos. At Sparsh Beauty Academy in Boliya, Shikha Jain trains you for real Indian wedding conditions—varied skin textures, hyperpigmentation, humid mandaps, heavy Rajwadi jewellery, and long wedding schedules—so your brides look timeless both in person and on camera.

  • Every core bridal & hair demo personally taught by Shikha Jain
  • Daily supervised hands-on practice on real skin tones & hair types
  • All practice makeup products provided inside the academy studio
  • Dedicated Rajwadi & Rajputi poshak, borla & odhani draping lab
  • Specialised module on Permanent Hair Straightening & Keratin care
  • Final portfolio photoshoot + Completion Certificate upon assessment

Pro Course Syllabus

Complete Curriculum: Skin to Stage-Ready Bride

Suitable for absolute beginners/freshers as well as practicing salon artists wanting to upgrade to luxury bridal & permanent hair standards.

  • Skin analysis (oily, dry, combination, acne-prone, textured & mature skin)
  • Cleansing, hydration layering, pore blurring & sweat-control priming
  • Precision colour correction for dark circles, acne marks & hyperpigmentation
  • Undertone matching across fair, medium, wheatish & deep Indian skin tones without grey cast
  • Cream & powder contouring, sculpted blush placement & transfer-resistant HD sealing
  • Eye shape correction (hooded, deep-set, round, almond & protruding eyes)
  • Soft bridal halo eyes, classic & colour smokey eyes, half/full cut-crease & shimmer foil
  • Smudged kohl waterline, clean wing liner & lower-lash gradation
  • Natural hair-like eyebrow shaping & undetectable false lash placement
  • Overlining balance, ombré bridal lips & long-wear matte/satin formulas
  • Traditional Rajwadi & Rajputi bridal aesthetics tailored for Malwa & Mewar weddings
  • Secure placement of borla, rakhdi, matha patti, heavy nath & aadh choker balance
  • Rajputi poshak odhani pinning, double-dupatta lehenga draping & classic saree pleats
  • Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Wedding & Reception look transitions
  • Foundational blow-dry, sectioning, crimping, ironing & volume waves
  • Traditional low bridal buns, floral/gajra styling, messy sangeet braids & extensions
  • Hair consultation & porosity check for chemical/smoothing services
  • Step-by-step demonstration of Permanent Hair Straightening, Keratin & Smoothing
  • Heat protection, neutralizer timing & client after-care counselling
  • Building a smart starter & luxury bridal vanity across budget and high-end brands
  • Hygiene, brush sanitization & client consultation workflow
  • Pricing packages, bridal contracts & Instagram reel/lighting showcase tips
  • Final look assessment + Portfolio Photoshoot (outfit, model & jewellery guidance provided)
  • Certificate of Completion from Sparsh Beauty Academy (internship opportunity based on studio requirement & performance)
