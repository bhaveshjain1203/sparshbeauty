About Shikha Jain

Bringing metropolitan bridal artistry, permanent hair science, and professional beauty education to Boliya, District Mandsaur.

Her Philosophy & Craft

Graceful Brides, Empowered Students

For Shikha Jain, beauty has never been about hiding someone beneath heavy layers—it is about honouring a bride’s own features, her heritage, and the quiet confidence she sees when she looks in the mirror.

Rooted in Boliya (District Mandsaur, Madhya Pradesh), Shikha built Sparsh Beauty Studio & Academy so women across the Malwa–Mewar region don’t have to travel to metro cities for luxury bridal finishing, safe permanent hair straightening, or career-grade academy mentorship.

From authentic Rajwadi & Rajputi bridal makeovers with traditional borla and odhani styling, to mirror-gloss permanent hair transformations and hands-on student batches, every service carries her personal eye for detail.

Collaborations & Recognition

Beauty Seminars, Model Shoots & Award Stages

Beyond daily studio brides, Shikha Jain actively participates in regional and national beauty seminars, award ceremonies, and curated editorial model photoshoots—continuously bringing fresh techniques back to her clients and students in Boliya.
